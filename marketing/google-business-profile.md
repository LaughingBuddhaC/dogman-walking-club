# Google Business Profile – The DogMan

Set it up at **https://business.google.com/create**, signed in with your Google account. It's free.
Everything below is ready to copy and paste.

## 1. Name and category
- **Business name:** `The DogMan`
  (Use only the real name. Google can suspend profiles that add keywords like "Hundeluftning Skovlunde" to the name.)
- **Primary category:** search for `Dog walker` / `Hundelufter` and pick the closest match.
- **Additional category (optional):** `Pet sitter` / `Dyrepasser`.

## 2. Location → choose **"No"** (no shop customers visit)
You go to the customers, so this is a *service-area business*. Google still asks for your address to verify you,
but it **stays hidden** from the public.

## 3. Service area
`Skovlunde`, `Ballerup`, `Herlev`, `Måløv`, `Glostrup`, `Rødovre`, `Albertslund`. Add or remove areas to match where you really walk.

## 4. Contact
- **Phone:** your mobile number
- **Website:** `https://dmw.cansasmaz.com`

## 5. Opening hours
Monday–Sunday **06:00–00:00**

## 9. Booking button
Under **Edit profile → Bookings**, add the link: `https://dmw.cansasmaz.com/#book`

## 10. Verification
Google usually asks service-area businesses to **record a short video** that shows:
- the area you work in (a street sign in Skovlunde, a local park)
- proof of the business (for example the flyer, your dog walking gear and leash, and the website on your phone)

Follow the steps in the app. Approval often takes a few days.

## After it's live
1. **Reviews are the #1 ranking factor.** After each of your first walks, send the customer the review link
   (Profile → *Ask for reviews*). Example text:
   `Tak fordi jeg måtte gå tur med [hund]! Vil du hjælpe mig med en kort anmeldelse? Det betyder meget for en ny lille virksomhed: [link]`
2. **Reply to every review**, even with just a short thank-you.
3. **Post once a week** (Profile → *Add update*): a photo from a walk and one line, e.g. "Ledige morgenture i uge 42 – book på dmw.cansasmaz.com".
